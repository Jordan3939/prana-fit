About The Studio

Where flexibility meets strength

Prana Fit Studio opened in 2016 with a simple idea: yoga and strength training shouldn't live in separate worlds. Our coaches are certified in both disciplines, so every program builds mobility and muscle together.

Whether you're recovering from injury, training for a sport, or just want to move better in daily life, our small-group format means real coaching, not just a playlist and a mirror.

  • Dual-certified yoga & strength coaches
  • Programming for every fitness level
  • In-studio and live virtual training

Why Train With Us

Results that go beyond the mirror

Combining yoga and strength training produces benefits neither delivers alone.

Functional Strength

Build muscle that transfers to real movement — lifting, carrying, climbing, and sport.

Joint Mobility

Mental Resilience
